- Recurrent-task improvement can conceal failure on **new directions/factors**, so these results do not prove plasticity is preserved either. They establish that end-of-task recurrent accuracy and feature rank alone cannot answer the intended question.

## 2. Which confounds and reproducibility gaps would reviewers identify?

### Takeaway
The central missing piece is a standardized **aged-versus-fresh learning test on a specified future-task distribution**, with task difficulty and head state controlled. Most intervention machinery exists, but locally there is no large-image causal intervention evidence. Several dormant runners contain measurement issues that should be fixed before producing paper numbers.

### Cited Findings
- Task streams carry all model weights and the head; the shared trainer constructs a new optimizer for every task. That is equivalent to retaining state for momentum-zero SGD, but changes Adam behavior and would also change momentum SGD. Optimizer-state handling must be an explicit condition in any optimizer comparison. [New optimizer](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/experiments/run_basic_task_training.py:42); [carryover explanation](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/experiments/run_tasks_stream.py:318).
- Logged train accuracy is accumulated **during** training as weights change, whereas test accuracy is evaluated after the epoch. It is not final-model training accuracy. For fine-grained capacity claims, re-evaluate the final checkpoint on train as well as held-out data. [Training accumulation](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/experiments/run_basic_task_training.py:56).
- Root summaries can be stale relative to completed tasks. CIFAR attributes root summary lists only tasks0–1 although task599 exists; CIFAR cluster ep40 root summary also lists only2 despite62 task summaries. ### Inferences
- Single-seed and recurring-task endpoint curves are insufficient for top-tier empirical claims about lost capacity, causal death, or a universal bottleneck.
- Separate optimization impairment, loss of positive transfer, catastrophic forgetting, and generalization loss. Training accuracy improving while test declines is a different phenomenon from failure to learn a matched fresh-solvable probe.
- Fixed training-pool silence can be absorbing for first-layer ReLUs under plain SGD, yet new samples/input transformations can revive them. This should be tested as a distribution-relative mechanism, with known literature overlap handled by the literature researcher. It should not be sold as a new observation by itself.
- Centering rescue does not prove a universal geometric trap: trainable negative biases can silence centered toy inputs, and bias, covariance, step size, initial margin and downstream gradient reach are competing mechanisms.

### Gaps
- No local broad validation of causal rescue, intervention effect reversal, gradient reach mediator, realistic-width/CNN robustness, or matched retention-cost advantage.
- Code versions used for saved runs are not established by merely reading current source. Cache validation checks settings and split lengths, not a full dataset/transform fingerprint; provenance should include source revision, exact split/permutation/task-bank hashes and optimizer policy. [Cache validation](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/data/pca_factors.py:157).
- Tests were not run and costly experiments were not started in this audit; this is evidence review, not certification of current executable behavior.

## 3. What experiment-led paper should be pursued, and what is the decisive pilot?

### Takeaway
With compute available, pursue **task-conditioned loss of plasticity and intervention response**: from the same aged checkpoint, geometry of the next task predicts both impairment relative to fresh and which intervention helps or harms. A high-value empirical contribution needs a predictive rule and causal, out-of-sample validation; another collection of dead-unit or rank correlations would not be sufficient. This is a research recommendation, not a finding from the saved runs.

### Cited Findings
There is already a new A–B–C adversarial task-search implementation; do **not** recommend inventing its basic protocol as new work. Its docstring says: raw normalized images remain inputs; binary labels threshold continuous PCA projection/cosine directions; B branches share a post-A checkpoint; disposable C probes have identical reset heads and minibatches; score is validation probe-learning cross entropy relative to pre-B; fresh B/C diagnose difficulty; confirmation uses independent directions and test images after selection. It explicitly warns that losing positive transfer is not necessarily worse-than-fresh plasticity. [Exact protocol](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/experiments/run_pca_adversarial.py:14).

The source constructs seeded candidate, selection, confirmation and random-control banks, selects B using selection damage, then probes prefix/selected/random/fresh controls and reports selected-minus-fresh as well as selected-minus-prefix/random. This is the strongest immediately usable infrastructure for an experiment-led continuation, but **no `results/pca_adversarial` output existed locally during the audit**. [Selection and controls](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/experiments/run_pca_adversarial.py:267); [confirmation comparisons](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/experiments/run_pca_adversarial.py:292). The design calls this a configurable starter and disclaims global optimality. [Design](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-pca-adversarial-design.md:19).

Existing code exposes reset-head/freeze-body utilities and arbitrary MLP widths, as well as CNNs; robot launchers explicitly propose single-task solvability before streams, with majority-class controls for odd-value factors. Thus broad validation is feasible with extensions, although not yet evidenced by saved results. [Head/reset](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/models/factory.py:140); [width/CNN factory](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/models/factory.py:41); [robot solvability](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/experiments/launch_scripts/launch_robot3d_single.sh:2).

### Inferences
Recommended estimand and experimental design (proposals):
1. At checkpoint age t, clone the same state for each held-out task C and intervention I. Primary outcome: integrated held-out learning loss over a fixed post-switch budget, relative to a matched fresh model. Report training loss/accuracy, test learning curve, final performance, steps-to-task-specific threshold and old-task retention separately. Run carried-head and paired-reset-head versions. This separates pre-existing readout mismatch from body adaptation.
2. Match fresh-C difficulty using the **whole fresh learning curve**, not only final accuracy. Equalize class balance, examples, updates, optimizer state and minibatch order. Keep the task bank and evaluation split independent of adversarial B selection. Include natural recurring tasks, unseen label directions, and genuinely new factors; do not let repeated tasks stand in for unseen-task plasticity.
3. Map angle/overlap between B and C, and geometry relative to the learned features, across many C directions at a **fixed checkpoint**. Compare candidate predictors against fresh-C difficulty, label correlation/task similarity, gradient interference, NTK target alignment, global feature rank, dead fraction, weight norm, and distance to initialization. Heterogeneity at one checkpoint by itself is not a contribution; a predictor that generalizes to new task banks, seeds, datasets and architectures is.
4. The decisive causal result is **intervention effect reversal at the same checkpoint**: e.g., partial reset or bias reactivation helps C1 but hurts equally fresh-solvable C2, predicted in advance by learned task geometry. Use a random-unit/sham intervention with matched parameter/output perturbation and an initial-function-change control. Quantify downstream gradient paths and unit revival, then explicitly block or reproduce the proposed mediator. Improve adaptation without trading away all previous performance.
5. Treat input/output drift as a factorial mechanism check: same label sequence under fixed input pool, resampled iid pool, and input permutations/other structured shifts. For MLP + plain SGD, add **function-transported permutations** (transform first-layer weights inversely so latent-coordinate activations are unchanged). Under the corresponding orthogonal coordinate map, this should match no-drift training up to numerical precision; it is a strong control for what an untransported permutation actually changes. Input-drift revival alone is already known and cannot carry novelty.

A decisive **1–2 week pilot** with compute available:
- Days1–2: freeze source/splits, recover remote run manifests if available, fix probe/evaluation semantics, reproduce the known negative controls. Start with MNIST/CIFAR A–B–C continuous-direction search already implemented, plus true Shapes3D factors. Use5–10 independent seeds and a sufficiently large untouched confirmation bank; choose task budgets from fresh curves, not a universal90% threshold.
- Days3–5: run selected-B, random-B, repeated-A/no-switch, equally hard stationary-task, prefix and fresh branches. Measure whether selected B produces **below-fresh** learning on confirmation C, not merely worse than a beneficial prefix. Report all candidates and selection-vs-confirmation gaps, including B trainability. Increase width from30 to120 and512 and sweep a reasonable log-spaced LR/budget grid; keep base data/labels paired.
- Days6–9: from a small set of impaired and non-impaired checkpoints, map response to head-only reset, partial body reset, bias-only reactivation, shrinking/perturbation, leaky activations/normalization controls, and available literature baselines. Fit a task-conditioned prediction on discovery banks, then freeze it. Test the predicted help/harm directions with matched shams and mediator ablation. Centering/offset dose experiments are useful diagnostics, not the headline.
- Days10–14: locked confirmation on a second image dataset and a materially different architecture (at least CNN or residual network, not width30 alone); corroborate on true factor-controlled Shapes3D and one fresh-solvable robot dataset if preprocessing is ready. Separate SGD versus Adam/momentum and optimizer-state policy. Hold out datasets/tasks from diagnostic fitting.
- **Go/no-go:** continue the major-paper route if below-fresh impairment is reproducible on unseen C, the proposed predictor beats difficulty/task-similarity and global metrics out of sample, and intervention benefit/harm is causally predicted and confirmed beyond the discovery setup. If only selected directions at width30/.1 toy SGD show it, keep a narrower paper/workshop scope. If B merely removes positive transfer, change the claim honestly rather than relabeling it LoP.

Minimal credible main-paper evidence (experiment-led, not minimal compute):
- Figure1: operational plasticity atlas showing both impairment and preserved/improved adaptation across task/drift settings, with fresh baselines.
- Figure2: damage and intervention-response maps for equally fresh-solvable future tasks at the same state; search and confirmation separate.
- Figure3: targeted causal intervention, matched shams, mediator necessity and sufficiency; distinguish global rank/death from task-specific reachability.
- Figure4: held-out predictor and intervention generalization across at least two non-toy data families, width/depth and a non-MLP architecture.
- Figure5/table: comparisons with well-tuned established plasticity interventions, adaptation–retention tradeoff and compute/parameter budget. Full per-seed runs and all nulls in appendix/release.

What to cut/defer: running every historical launch set without a decisive hypothesis; treating robot dataset preparation as a contribution; the memorization-vs-representation taxonomy as a separate main story; old NTK/norm toys without a clear link to the validated effect; a claim that rank reduction or inactive units universally cause LoP; arbitrary90% collapse thresholds; a benchmark contribution consisting only of raw-pixel PCA bins. Keep negative controls and fresh-solvability failures in the evidence, because they delimit the claim.

### Gaps
The pilot can decide whether this empirical paper exists; it cannot guarantee publication or that the hypothesized geometry/intervention relation will survive. Compute availability removes a throughput constraint, not the need for identifiable effects, novelty relative to current work, and rigorous confirmation. The current local image results alone are not submission-ready for ICML/NeurIPS/ICLR, but the infrastructure is far enough along that a focused two-week empirical decision is realistic.
