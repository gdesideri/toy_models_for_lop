# Toy mechanisms and theory audit

## What is actually established by the saved toy experiments?

### Takeaway

The strongest observed mechanism is **finite-training-pool absorption with negative hidden biases**. The saved toy runs do not support a general claim that mean-shift-induced rotation into a population-inactive cone is the dominant source of plasticity loss. The most useful next paper would make future-support dependence a causal, experiment-led question, rather than make another general dead-neuron or rank-collapse claim.

### Inferences

- The cleanest supported observation is stronger than a correlation with dead-unit counts: after the observed collapse in the raw example, all hidden training features coincide at zero, so no refitted scalar readout can separate training examples with opposite labels. This directly explains the observed failure on the fixed pool. It does not establish impossibility on the population or a mechanism unique to continual learning. This follows from the saved zero activations and model architecture above.
- Negative bias is necessary to silence nonzero ReLUs on a centered full-dimensional symmetric population support, but the existing event label `bias_driven` does not mean a bias-only counterfactual would kill the unit. Rotation and bias changes can jointly produce silence. The support inequality makes this distinction exact. — [Geometry](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/src/unit_geometry.py:35), [event classifier](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/experiments/run_orthogonal_tasks.py:557).
- Existing data favor making **sample reuse and future activation coverage** the proposed explanatory variable. Input mean alone is insufficient: the strongest verified collapse has mean zero, and the units remain population-active.

- No paired checkpoint experiment yet shows that changing finite-pool coverage, while controlling optimizer state and current function, causes recovery. Current artifacts establish absorption and compatible geometry, not that refreshing data will rescue these checkpoints quickly.
- The current current-task readout probe cache was created over an earlier inventory. ## Which mathematical claims survive, and which should be abandoned?

### Takeaway

Abandon the antipodal-gradient-cancellation capacity theorem. Replace it with a precise absorption/support statement as a diagnostic lemma, not as the headline contribution. The invalid theorem is isolated in an April design/implementation plan; I found no implemented proof notebook or use of that identity in the current runner.

### Cited Findings

- The April spec correctly notices that two opposite parameter gradients can sum to zero under symmetry, then incorrectly concludes the pair is frozen in function space and the network loses two units of capacity. It also says a manually frozen antipodal pair must have individual gradient norms near zero; freezing parameters does not imply their loss gradients vanish. — [Invalid consequence](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-04-22-gradient-flat-proof-design.md:10), [invalid frozen-pair gradient expectation](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-04-22-gradient-flat-proof-design.md:69).
- **Explicit counterexample, analytic derivation and tiny read-only autograd check:** Let `x∈{-1,+1}` uniformly, `y=|x|=1`, `w+=(.5)`, `w-=(-.5)`, equal readouts `v+=v-=1`, and biases zero. Then `f=.5|x|`, MSE `L=.25`, and gradients are `∂L/∂w+=-.5`, `∂L/∂w-=+.5`. Their sum is zero, but one gradient step changes the function to `(.5+.5η)|x|`. Bias gradients are both `-.5`; readout gradients both `-.25`. Under uniform `[-1,1]`, full-MSE hidden-weight gradients instead are `(a-1)/3` and `-(a-1)/3`, still individually nonzero for `a≠1`. These counterexamples satisfy the claimed symmetry/even-target assumptions. They refute the inference from parameter-gradient cancellation to zero functional update in the spec. — [Assumptions being refuted](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-04-22-gradient-flat-proof-design.md:10), [actual trainable ReLU form](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/models/toy.py:35).
- At a fixed dataset, full-batch gradient flow has `df/dt = -J J^T ∇_f L`. Functional stasis requires `J^T ∇_f L=0`; the sum of two parameter-block gradients is irrelevant. Opposite parameter gradients can move opposite neurons together and change `|w·x|`. The repository already implements the full Jacobian/NTK, making the correct diagnostic available. — [Jacobian and kernel implementation](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/src/ntk.py:31).
- A correct replacement lemma: in a first-layer ReLU network with fixed training inputs, if `w_i·x_n+b_i≤0` for every training example and ReLU'(0)=0, then the gradients of `w_i,b_i,a_i` vanish for **any subsequent labels**. Under ordinary SGD without momentum, weight decay, injected noise, or parameter coupling, that unit remains silent forever. This follows directly from the implemented derivative formulas. The extension to deeper layers is invalid if their upstream representations move. — [Derivative formulas](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/experiments/run_orthogonal_tasks.py:303), [plain SGD defaults](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/experiments/run_orthogonal_tasks.py:284), [fixed train sampling](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/experiments/run_orthogonal_tasks.py:319).
- A second correct lemma: for support `S={μ+Au:||u||≤1}`, a unit is population-inactive iff `w·μ+||A^T w||+b≤0`. For `μ=0,b=0,A` invertible, this is impossible for nonzero `w`. Input centering removes a bias-free population-inactive cone, but trainable negative biases restore inactive regions and a finite sample can miss a positive activation cap. — [Exact formula](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/src/unit_geometry.py:1).
- **Old rotating/norm toy status:** current runners are Adam-trained, four-unit bias-free ReLU regressors on iid Gaussian inputs with linear targets and trainable signed readouts. Their sweeps are configured, but expected local JSON and NTK artifacts are absent. Scaling the old target by 20 changes function amplitude, readout, residual magnitude and Adam history, not only hidden norms; conditional on exact fit, the new orthogonal target starts at MSE 401 instead of the zero predictor's MSE 1. Rotating snapshots omit the readout, and NTK reconstruction falls back to a random readout if absent. These are sources/capabilities, not empirical demonstrations of a norm obstruction. — [Norm phases](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/experiments/run_norm_growth_270.py:123), [rotating snapshots](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/experiments/run_rotating_target.py:111), [existing comprehensive older-source audit](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/results/toy_audit_20261028/older_toys_source_diagnostics.json:1), [missing artifacts inventory](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/results/toy_audit_20261028/older_toys_audit.json).

### Inferences

- Do not use norm growth or NTK eigenvalue shrinkage as the main claim. ReLU reparameterization `w→c w,a→a/c` preserves the function and gates while changing norms and the Euclidean NTK. For width4/input2, homogeneity gives at most eight independent scalar-output Jacobian directions; an N=64 Gram matrix necessarily has many zero eigenvalues before any LoP. Adam further breaks a literal unpreconditioned-NTK convergence-rate interpretation. These are architectural deductions from the cited model and Jacobian definitions.
- Treat the correct lemmas as guardrails for experimental interpretation. Dead-unit invariance and support inactivity are unlikely to provide the distinguishing novelty on their own; the literature agent is assessing overlap.

### Gaps

- I found no proof notebook outside the abandoned plan. Search hits in runnable source are an output-cancellation *metric*, not the antipodal theorem. The current orthogonal implementation's analytic gradients are independent of it. — [Current gradient code](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/experiments/run_orthogonal_tasks.py:297), [metric](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/experiments/run_orthogonal_tasks.py:463).

## What experiment-led contribution would be worth pursuing now?

### Takeaway

My preferred **hypothesis**, not present finding: **the effect of a plasticity intervention depends on the support and feature requirements of future tasks; dormancy on historical examples can be an absorbing bottleneck or recoverable capacity.** The paper must identify a reproducible regime boundary or intervention-effect reversal across future tasks, then validate it on substantial image tasks and multiple architectures. A narrow claim that dead ReLUs hurt learning is insufficient.

### Cited Findings

- The toy setup is especially suitable for support-level manipulation because it separates `μ,A,u`, defines exact population support, keeps task identity explicit, and logs both continued and fresh states. Its current control pairs use the same random weights rather than function-matched initializations. — [Task family](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/tasks/orthogonal_halfspaces.py:152), [same-weights control](/Users/ariv18uq/Desktop/toy_models_for_lop/plasticity/experiments/run_orthogonal_tasks.py:488).
- The current raw example provides the specific signature needed for a finite-pool versus population distinction: `max_train preactivation≤0` yet `max_support preactivation>0`, with zero empirical parameter gradients and nonzero held-out activation frequency. This is directly observed above and should be measured on independent confirmation inputs across the eventual image experiments.

### Inferences / proposed experiments (none are completed results)

1. **Primary paired experiment:** Restore identical trained checkpoints, then branch into fixed historical pool, independent pool from the same population, continuously refreshed iid samples, and a duplicate-of-old-pool control. Fix total updates, batch size, target rule and optimizer state. Increasing the number of new support points should help sample-silent/population-active units, whereas merely repeating old points cannot. Population-certified units provide an explicit negative-control stratum. This is a causal support-access assay, not another correlation of accuracy with dead counts.
2. **Core measurable variable:** For unit i at checkpoint θ, estimate `p_i(P)=Pr_{x~P}(w_i·x+b_i>0)` on independent inputs. Record `p_i(history)`, `p_i(future)`, empirical max-preactivation margin, and whether reachable features separate the prospective labels. A useful potential signature is historical sample silence with positive future activation mass. In the ball toy, the normalized threshold `t_i=-(w_i·μ+b_i)/||A^T w_i||` determines population activation mass exactly. For t near1, cap probability scales as `(1-t)^((D+1)/2)`. For a **fixed unit independent of an iid pool**, miss probability is `(1-p_i)^N`; do not apply this naively to adaptively trained weights without a uniform bound or independent confirmation pool.
3. **Future-support intervention interaction:** From the same historical checkpoint, evaluate tasks with unchanged support, support rotated/shifted toward dormant activation caps, and support rotated away, with balanced target difficulty and fresh-C controls. Compare gate-opening repair, optimizer reset, readout-only refit, norm-only rescaling, and full reset. Seek a pre-specified interaction: repair helps under one future-support regime and offers no help or harms useful transfer under another. This interaction, if broad and reproducible, is a more distinctive paper than unit death alone.
4. **Minimal causal repair at fixed training predictions:** For a train-silent unit, zero its readout and move only its bias to a specified historical activation quantile. Its contribution to all old training predictions remains exactly zero initially, while a learning path opens. Compare readout-zero-only, bias-only, and sham perturbations, plus repairs matched for initial output disturbance and parameter-distance. This is an assay, not a claimed new general algorithm. Multiple dead units can be repaired with a fixed budget. Measure paired learning curves, time to a fresh-controlled threshold and acquisition area under curve.
5. **Protect against task-selection overfitting:** Use one target/support bank to identify candidate mechanisms, freeze all choices, then evaluate independent target/support banks and seeds. If an adversarially chosen future task is used, report matched random-task results and a fresh network on every selected task. This is essential given current large-stream results reportedly mostly improve.
6. **Architecture transfer:** Establish the same predicted intervention interaction on MLPs and convolutional ReLU models, then test activation/layer-normalization and residual-network variants as mechanism-negative or altered-mechanism cases. Measure activations on the actual historical and future datasets. First-layer absorption is exact under the stated conditions; deeper-layer inactivity is conditional on moving upstream representations, and activation support must be tracked dynamically rather than called permanent.
7. **Clean centering/anisotropy control:** With hidden biases, translation or invertible input transforms preserve the hypothesis class if weights/biases are transformed appropriately. Function-match checkpoint states (`b_shift=b_center−Wμ`, and `W'=WT^{-1}` under x'=Tx) before comparing training. Ordinary Euclidean SGD is coordinate-dependent, so include the corresponding metric/preconditioning control. For bias-free nets, centering changes the function class relative to the data; a rescue cannot simply be called a pure geometry effect. Current thin-x1/thin-x3 contrast needs the target held fixed in round coordinates to be a clean nuisance-axis experiment.

### Gaps / main failure mode / stop list

- **Main failure mode:** all compelling absorption evidence is in tiny fixed-pool ReLU toys; refreshed data may rescue only a contrived regime, and modern architectures or broad image streams may show no meaningful intervention interaction. If the broad confirmatory experiment fails, the general paper claim must be dropped rather than defended with more small networks.
- **Stop pursuing as headline:** the false antipodal capacity theorem; a required 270-degree rotation of signed-readout networks; target-amplitude scaling as a clean norm intervention; raw NTK/rank correlations; mean centering as a universal cure; a benchmark expansion whose streams do not create a documented acquisition deficit against fresh controls.
- **Recommended conclusion if the interaction is confirmed:** a paper about *when plasticity interventions work*, determined by future support and accessible feature requirements. If no such interaction survives broad controls, the defensible endpoint is a limited diagnostic/benchmark paper or workshop report, not an asserted universal mechanism.
